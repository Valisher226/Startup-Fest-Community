STARTUP FEST PLATFORM: DEMO v4

How to open
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. No internet or installation is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

How to use
- Use "Viewing as" (top right) to switch between Participant, Mentor, Investor, Company and Org team.
- Everything you click (registrations, applications, gigs, messages) is saved only in your own browser.

All people, startups, companies and numbers are sample data, except 14 regions and the $410K in Catalyst deals.

New in v4
- The design now matches the website (startupfest.vercel.app): its fonts, colours, floating menu, red and black tiles, lightning-star motif and the "Catalyst" hero.
- Sign-up ("Ro‘yhatdan o‘tish") uses the same fields as the website's priority list: programme, name, region, Telegram, email, phone, track, experience level, motivation.
- Plans (footer → Plans): tiers for companies, SMEs and scouting partners, with a mock Click / Payme / bank-transfer checkout. Nothing is charged.
- Guild pages (Community → click a guild): member companies, open briefs, pilot results.
- Partner panel (as Company): membership, return on membership, invoices (pay or download).
- Org dashboard: revenue (yearly membership revenue, invoices paid and due, success fees) and "Mark paid".
- Shortlist requests now create a 5M UZS sourcing-fee invoice, unless the partner has a scouting retainer.

Earlier versions: Uzbek/Russian/English, the season and National Final, regional councils, startup profiles with consent, Startup Earn lifecycle, CSV export (v3); sign-up per stakeholder, points and badges, event check-in by ID (try SF-27-00317 as Org team), accept/decline, 100-day pilots, notifications, Save/Load scenario (v2).
