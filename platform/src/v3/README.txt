STARTUP FEST PLATFORM: DEMO v3

How to open
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. No internet or installation is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

How to use
- Use "Viewing as" (top right) to switch between Participant, Mentor, Investor, Company and Org team.
- Everything you click (registrations, applications, gigs, messages) is saved only in your own browser.

All people, startups, companies and numbers are sample data, except 14 regions and the $410K in Catalyst deals.

New in v3
- Language switch at the top: O‘zbekcha (default), Русский, English. Uzbek and Russian are draft translations; review them with a native editor before launch.
- Season (Mavsum): campus round → regional stage → National Final, regional leaderboard by track, final line-up with Startup Weekend wildcards. As Org team, pick a finalist per region.
- Regional council pages (Community → click a region): seats by sector with the "max 2 per sector" rule, a local problem bank, "Turn into a brief".
- Startup profiles (Startups → click a name) with an "Open to recommendations" switch. Startups that switch it off cannot be picked for shortlists.
- Shortlist requests now have a status (Requested → Curating → Sent) that the Org team moves forward.
- Startup Earn lifecycle: Open → Assigned → Delivered → Paid, with a rating. Earnings show on the participant's ID and count as value to members.
- Org dashboard: export regions, the Catalyst pipeline and the season leaderboard as CSV files.

Also in v2
- Sign-up per stakeholder, points and badges from real actions, event check-in by ID (try SF-27-00317 as Org team), brief pages with accept/decline, the 100-day pilot workspace, notifications, Save/Load scenario, Reset demo.
