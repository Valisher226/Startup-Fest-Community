STARTUP FEST PLATFORM: DEMO v2

How to open
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. No internet or installation is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

How to use
- Use "Viewing as" (top right) to switch between Participant, Mentor, Investor, Company and Org team.
- Everything you click (registrations, applications, gigs, messages) is saved only in your own browser.

All people, startups, companies and numbers are sample data, except 14 regions and the $410K in Catalyst deals.

New in v2
- Sign up per stakeholder: "Get a Startup Fest ID" asks different questions for each role.
- Points, levels and badges come from what you do (register, check in, apply, post, record a contract).
- Event pages. As Org team, open an event and check people in by typing an ID (try SF-27-00317).
- Catalyst brief pages. As Company, accept or decline applicants with a note.
- 100-day pilot workspace: milestones, weekly updates and the contract value.
- Notifications (bell icon) for every role.
- Footer: Roadmap, Save scenario / Load scenario (share a demo state as a file), Reset demo.
