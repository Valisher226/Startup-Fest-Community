STARTUP FEST PLATFORM: DEMO v6

How to open
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. No internet or installation is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

How to sign in
- The demo opens on the Sign in page. Every member type has its own login: Participant, Mentor, Investor, Company, Small business, Scouting partner and Org team.
- The login and password for each type are in LOGINS.txt (in this folder). "Fill the demo login" on the Sign in page fills them in for you.
- A login only works on its own page: a mentor's login on the Investor page is refused with a link to the right page.
- The menu with your name (top right) has My ID, Account settings (change email or password), Switch account and Log out.
- Everything you do is saved only in your own browser.

New in v6
- Create account: a step-by-step sign-up like the Common App. Step 1 is choosing your member type, then: create your account (email, password with live rules), personal information, a profile for your type, questions in your own words, consent and privacy (with a parent or guardian step for founders under 18), and review and submit. A progress bar and a list of sections show what is done; answers are saved as you go and any section can be edited from the review.
- Two new member types: Small business (pays for access to startups and student builders) and Scouting partner (asks Startup Fest to recommend startups, pays a sourcing fee).
- Office hours: founders book free 30-minute sessions with mentors; mentors open or close slots in a weekly grid, mark sessions done with notes; founders rate them. The Org team sees every booking.
- Judging: mentors score teams at regional stages on 5 weighted criteria; a live leaderboard averages all judges. The Org team sees which judges are done, sends reminders, and locks results, which sends the top team per track to the National Final.
- Peer groups of 8: a paid tier (150 000 so'm a month, free for Champions and regional finalists) with a moderator, waiting lists when full, and shared session notes. Payment creates an invoice.
- Champions and Fellows: requirements and perks, applications with two references, Org team review, yearly renewals, and a Hall of Champions.
- Each member type's home page has a "for you" strip; the Org dashboard has a Programmes block.

All people, startups, companies and numbers are sample data, except 14 regions and the $410K in Catalyst deals.
