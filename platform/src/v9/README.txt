STARTUP FEST PLATFORM: DEMO v9

How to open
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. No internet or installation is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

How to sign in
- The demo opens on the Sign in page. Every member type has its own login: Participant, Mentor, Investor, Company, Small business, Scouting partner and Org team.
- The login and password for each type are in LOGINS.txt (in this folder). "Fill the demo login" on the Sign in page fills them in for you.
- A login only works on its own page: a mentor's login on the Investor page is refused with a link to the right page.
- The menu with your name (top right) has My ID, Account settings (change email or password), Switch account and Log out.
- Everything you do is saved only in your own browser.

New in v9: Telegram, tickets and payments
- Telegram (menu with your name → Telegram): a phone with the Startup Fest bot. Notifications arrive as bot messages with an "Open" button; commands /events, /myid, /tickets, /digest, /help reply in the chat; the "Mini-app" tab shows your ID with QR, your next ticket and quick links. Accounts without Telegram can link a username.
- "Sign in with Telegram" on the Sign in page signs in any account that linked Telegram (Aziza, Bekzod and the Org team are linked already).
- Paid tickets: VC Battle (Standard, Student, Investor lounge), the Catalyst showcase partner seat and the Startup Fest School course. Checkout in 3 steps: ticket type, number and promo code (try STUDENT50, CHAMPION or STARTUPNIGHT) → Click, Payme, Uzcard or Humo card (a demo card number is shown) → SMS code (shown on screen). The ticket gets its own QR, appears in "My tickets" and on the event page, and can be refunded.
- Offline check-in (Org team, event page): switch on "Offline mode" and check-ins are saved on the device with a "waiting to sync" counter; "Sync now" sends them when the internet is back. Ticket codes like T-5521 work at the desk; a used ticket is refused.
- Notification settings: choose Telegram, email, SMS or in-platform for each kind of message.
- Payments (Org team): money received, ticket income, fees per payment method, refunds, and every ticket and invoice in one table.

Added in v8: trust, permissions and personal data
- Security at sign-in: 5 wrong passwords lock that login for 15 minutes; suspended accounts cannot sign in; Org team accounts need a 6-digit code after the password (the demo shows the code on screen); if "Keep me signed in" is off, you are signed out after 30 minutes without activity.
- Org team → Dashboard → "Trust and safety" (also in the menu with your name):
  - Users: every account, search and filter by member type, invite a member (gets a temporary password and must change it), suspend or reactivate, reset a password.
  - Permissions: a table of features × member types. Untick a box and that page disappears from the menu and direct links are blocked.
  - Audit log: sign-ins, failed logins, permission changes, consents, data downloads, deletions, payments, programme decisions; filter and export CSV.
  - Backend plan: what the real platform needs (database in Uzbekistan, Telegram login, hashed passwords, server-side permissions) and a monthly cost estimate.
- Privacy centre (menu with your name): what we store about you, consent switches with history, "Download my data" (JSON), and "Delete my account" for accounts you created yourself.
- Account settings: two-step code switch and recent sign-ins.
- Event check-in: "Scan with the camera" reads the QR on a member's ID in Chrome on Android or a Mac; other browsers explain and keep typing the ID.

Added in v7: investors and capital
- Investor login → "Deal flow": every startup that is raising (round, ask, terms, how much is already committed), a pipeline from Watching to Invested, watch-lists, "My intros" with statuses, and "My portfolio".
- Verified Investor Programme: investors apply (type, track record, ID check, code of conduct). Only verified investors can open data rooms. The Org team verifies or declines on "Capital".
- Deal room per startup: the investor requests access → the founder approves → the investor signs an NDA → documents (download), questions and answers, and a soft commitment. "Log investment" records the deal.
- Founder side (Participant login → My ID → Fundraising): the round summary, an investor-readiness checklist, uploading documents, approving or declining access requests, answering questions, and seeing commitments. Founders without a round can open one.
- VC Battle (12 Dec): four rounds; investor teams split a $1M virtual budget; members vote live, once per round; the Org team opens and closes voting, can "Simulate the hall", moves rounds and announces the winner.
- Logged investments are added to "value to members" on the Org dashboard (at 12 800 so'm per dollar).

Added in v6
- Create account: a step-by-step sign-up like the Common App. Step 1 is choosing your member type, then: create your account (email, password with live rules), personal information, a profile for your type, questions in your own words, consent and privacy (with a parent or guardian step for founders under 18), and review and submit. A progress bar and a list of sections show what is done; answers are saved as you go and any section can be edited from the review.
- Two new member types: Small business (pays for access to startups and student builders) and Scouting partner (asks Startup Fest to recommend startups, pays a sourcing fee).
- Office hours: founders book free 30-minute sessions with mentors; mentors open or close slots in a weekly grid, mark sessions done with notes; founders rate them. The Org team sees every booking.
- Judging: mentors score teams at regional stages on 5 weighted criteria; a live leaderboard averages all judges. The Org team sees which judges are done, sends reminders, and locks results, which sends the top team per track to the National Final.
- Peer groups of 8: a paid tier (150 000 so'm a month, free for Champions and regional finalists) with a moderator, waiting lists when full, and shared session notes. Payment creates an invoice.
- Champions and Fellows: requirements and perks, applications with two references, Org team review, yearly renewals, and a Hall of Champions.
- Each member type's home page has a "for you" strip; the Org dashboard has a Programmes block.

All people, startups, companies and numbers are sample data, except 14 regions and the $410K in Catalyst deals.
