STARTUP FEST PLATFORM: DEMO v5

How to open
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. No internet or installation is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

How to use
- Use "Viewing as" (top right) to switch between Participant, Mentor, Investor, Company and Org team.
- Everything you click (registrations, applications, gigs, messages) is saved only in your own browser.

All people, startups, companies and numbers are sample data, except 14 regions and the $410K in Catalyst deals.

New in v5: the content engine
- As Org team, open "Kontent / Content".
- This week's posts: pick what goes into the Monday digest (you can add your own items), the Wednesday problem of the week, and the Thursday member launches.
- Telegram preview in Uzbek, Russian or English, with a character count and "Copy text". "Mark as published" updates "This week" on every member's home page.
- Members (Participant view) can "Share a launch" from the home page; the Org team approves it into Thursday's post.
- Calendar and reach: the next 4 weeks of posts (published, draft, planned), views per post as a chart, and a form to log views.
- Instagram cards: 4 templates in the Startup Fest style; edit the text and "Download PNG" (1080 × 1350).
