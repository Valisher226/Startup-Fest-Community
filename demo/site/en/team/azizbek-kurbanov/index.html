Azizbek Kurbanov

Role: CEO, AK Capital Consultancy Firm · Strategic Advisor, Yoshlar Ventures

Focus: Pitching, fundraising and advisory to VC funds and accelerators

About me

Azizbek has spent a decade building companies and supporting entrepreneurs in e-commerce, insurance, fintech and renewable energy. He was CEO of United Ventures, a $40M fund set up by seven state banks, and co-founded and co-led Sug'urta Bozor. AK Capital now helps VC funds build startup pipelines and connects founders with investors. He studied Economics at Westminster International University in Tashkent (first-class honours) and completed executive programmes at Cornell and Yale.
