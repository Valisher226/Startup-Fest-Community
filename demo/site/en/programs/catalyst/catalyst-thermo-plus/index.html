Catalyst x Thermo Plus

Thermo Plus, a basalt insulation plant in Khorezm, produces 30,000 tonnes a year and covers 38% of Uzbekistan’s demand. Startups studied the plant from the inside and worked on automating its calculations and building digital services for its customers.

Teams that signed partnership agreements

On 11 November, 30 participants in 8 startup teams toured the plant, analysed its real problems and presented their solutions to the plant’s leadership and a jury. Four teams signed partnership agreements with the plant; the contract amounts were not disclosed.
