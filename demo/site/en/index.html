Empowering Uzbekistan's Next Generation of Builders

Startup Fest exists to democratize technology and entrepreneurship for youth across Uzbekistan. We bridge the gap between ambitious school students and real-world execution — providing the tools, mentorship, and platform needed to turn bold ideas into scalable startups, autonomous robots, and breakthrough software.

“This is where the dreams begin.”
